Basis & assumptions

  1. One total, two halves. The invoice adds up everything that came in and everything that went out; net profit is the difference, and the partner share is 25% of that. There are no sub-totals to reconcile — every line on the page is already inside net profit exactly once.
  2. Ticket sales = completed bookings only (tickets delivered), by completion date in June, Singapore time. Refunded/cancelled bookings excluded.
  3. Price per ticket is derived, not a list price — a blended average. Actual charges vary by rider: standard fares alongside custom friends-&-family, beta-tester and bug-tester rates.
  4. FX — blended actual. 0.31977 SGD/RM = total SGD paid to top up the KTMB card ÷ RM received, across all June top-ups. The pending 1 Jul top-up is excluded (next month, unsettled).
  5. Processing fee — blended actual. 5.3347% = June Airwallex payment fees ÷ gross deposits, applied to each route's revenue. Refund fees (SGD 18.50) excluded — they relate to refunded bookings, not sold tickets.
  6. Infrastructure is a flat SGD 500.00/month shared cost, not attributable to a single route, so it is deducted once at the total level.
  7. Deposits ≠ revenue. SGD 35,483.50 was collected in deposits; the excess over revenue is unspent rider wallet balance (a liability) and is excluded.
  8. Terminated bookings (174). Ticket was bought then cancelled: rider is refunded 50% (you keep 50%) and KTMB refunds 50% of the fare. Net = ½ revenue kept − ½ fare = +SGD 543.02. The 50% KTMB refund is assumed — the actual amount is not stored in our system (only in the KTMB eWallet); confirm there for an exact figure.
  9. Wasted processing fee. SGD 1,095.00 was withdrawn by riders in June; the inbound Airwallex fee on that money (5.3347%) earned no ticket revenue, so SGD 58.42 is booked as cost.
  10. Profit-share split. The agreed 50% marketing share (SGD 8,271.17) is divided equally between 2 partners at 25% each. This invoice bills one 25% share; the net profit and all workings are identical for both.
